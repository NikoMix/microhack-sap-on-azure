## Learning objectives

By the end of this lab a trainee can:

- Explain the eligible attribution types (**DPOR / PAL / CSP**) and the ≥ 3 unique customer rule.
- Use a Copilot skill to turn the By-Customer consumption export into an **anonymised customer list**
  and a **cross-reference table** linking each customer to a B.1.1 case study and B.3.1 skilling holder.
- Identify ineligible cases (e.g. government subscriptions via PAL) and the fix.

## Lab outline

<Steps>
  1. Review the sample By-Customer export. 2. Build the customer list + attribution evidence with
  the `build-customer-crossref` skill. 3. Cross-reference to case studies and skilling holders.
</Steps>
